# Play Store Listing — EasyTeam CRM (com.primafisoft.etcrm)

## Short description (max 80 chars)

## Full description (max 4000 chars)
EasyTeam CRM puts your entire sales pipeline in your pocket.

Manage contacts, companies, and deals on the go, reply to WhatsApp, Messenger, and Instagram conversations from a single inbox, and keep your team's tasks and follow-ups on track — all synced live with your EasyTeam CRM workspace.

KEY FEATURES
• Contacts & Companies — view and update customer records from anywhere
• Deal Pipeline — track deal stages, values, and next steps
• Unified Messaging — WhatsApp, Messenger, and Instagram in one inbox, auto-linked to the right contact and deal
• Tasks & Reminders — never miss a follow-up
• Push Notifications — get notified the moment a lead messages you
• Voice & Audio — send voice notes and make calls directly from WhatsApp conversations

Built for teams who sell and support customers over chat. EasyTeam CRM is the mobile companion to the EasyTeam CRM web app.

Requires an existing EasyTeam CRM workspace account.
